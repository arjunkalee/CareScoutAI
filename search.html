Questions

How do I start?

Enter a facility name or location. The search pulls from federal nursing home records and surfaces both standard ratings and hidden risks that matter to your decision.

What do the filters show?

Filters let you narrow by staffing patterns, safety citations, clinical assessments, and other factors. Each filter reveals what traditional ratings miss—like weekend shortages.

What are hidden risks?

Hidden risks are patterns our AI identifies in raw federal data, including gaps in RN coverage during specific shifts.
